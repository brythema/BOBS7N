The Believer's Standard

BOBS7N is a luxury streetwear brand that merges premium craftsmanship with bold contemporary design. Every piece is handcrafted in Nigeria and created for individuals who embrace confidence, individuality, and timeless style. Inspired by modern culture, architecture, and high fashion, BOBS7N delivers elevated essentials with meticulous attention to detail, premium fabrics, and limited production runs. Our mission is to redefine African luxury streetwear for a global audience while maintaining authenticity, exclusivity, and exceptional quality.

Mission Statement

To create world-class luxury streetwear that empowers self-expression through exceptional craftsmanship, innovative design, and uncompromising quality.
